Adapted from snowch/event-streaming-lab and snowch/assembly-lab.
Before drafting, record practical outcomes, prerequisites, likely misconceptions,
small complete examples and fresh application tasks in docs/learning-design.md.
Define terms before use. Keep one main learning outcome per lesson. Use inspectable
inputs, prediction, actual execution, one deliberate change and a transfer question.
Provide progressive hints and explanatory feedback. Run every published example
from its documented initial state. Distinguish simulated, invented and measured
data. Cite primary sources and record checks and limitations in docs/review.md.
After implementation, review the essential route with optional panels closed,
representation, state, feedback and cognitive load. Browser checks and author
review do not establish learning effectiveness. No additional approval is required.
